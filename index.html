About Me

I am an assistant professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences and Center for Machine Learning Research at Peking University.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Princeton University and the University of Pennsylvania. I received my Ph.D. in computational mathematics from Peking University in 2018, advised by Prof. Weinan E, and my B.S. in mathematics from Nankai University in 2012.

I aim to develop a predictive science of deep learning by combining mathematical analysis with large-scale training experiments. My current focus is on scaling laws and LLM pretraining. For more, see my research overview and talk slides.

Recruiting

We welcome postdocs, PhD students, and undergraduates interested in the scientific foundations of AI. I recruit PhD students through both the School of Mathematical Sciences and the Center for Machine Learning Research (CMLR).

If you are interested in joining us, please email me your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
