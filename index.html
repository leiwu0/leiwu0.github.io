About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences and Center for Machine Learning Research at Peking University.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Princeton University and the University of Pennsylvania. I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Peking University in 2018, advised by Prof. Weinan E, and my B.S. in Mathematics from Nankai University in 2012.

I aim to develop a predictive science of deep learning, connecting mathematical analysis of tractable models with empirical studies of large-scale training. My current focus is on understanding and improving LLM pretraining. See Research for details.
